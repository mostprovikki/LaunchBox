Redesign — every page and state

Static mockups with one consistent sample moment (Sat 1 Aug, 09:41). Dark is the default; the toggle in the bar persists. Start at the Overview and click through — the nav is real.
What is new vs the current app — the Overview tab itself, the next-24h schedule, the needs-attention feed, the daemon-unreachable and token-invalid banners, and the whole Sessions tab. All of it renders data the daemon already has; no new backend concepts. History is renamed Runs. The IA (Overview + 5 tabs) is the proposal you chose provisionally — judge it by clicking through.
