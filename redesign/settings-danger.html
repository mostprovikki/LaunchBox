Danger zone

Cleanup — wipe all jobs, runs and logs Every job definition, the entire run history and all log files are deleted. Settings, projects and sessions stay. There is no undo. Type cleanup to arm the button, then approve with Touch ID.
Uninstall LaunchBox Stops and removes the launchd agent, deletes ~/.claude-scheduler including this UI, and removes the binary. Your repos, beads and Claude sessions are untouched. Type uninstall to arm, then approve with Touch ID.
The 5-minute approval grace window never applies to these two actions — every click re-asks.
